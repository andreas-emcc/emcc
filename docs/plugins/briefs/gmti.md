## 1. Purpose

Brings **GMTI** (ground moving target indication) radar data into emc2 and sends the simulator's simulated GMTI as STANAG 4607. Server part only, no web part: an admin sets up its links in the Settings list.

- **In** (ADR-0048 point 3): a link reads 4607 packets and writes into its target layer **the sensor platform** as a symbol, **the dwell area** and, from T-158, **the sensor's coverage** (the job's bounding area) as shapes, and **each target report as a detection** (data type `gmti.detection`), paired with its platform (ADR-0051 point 8). Detections are not tracks; they fade and go by their data type's expiry.
- **Out** (ADR-0048 points 4 and 5): a link is an output channel that takes **sensor reports only** and sends each sensor report it gets as a packet. In M5 the sender is the simulator's GMTI sensor. It takes no objects from layers, and does not relay what it receives.

Task T-141 builds it, by PCA-gmti.

## 2. Specification

- **STANAG 4607 Edition 4:** AEDP-4607 Edition A, Version 1 (the format), with AEDP-4607.1 Edition A, Version 1 (the implementation guide), ADR-0048 point 1. Public NSDD documents in a local folder; read there, never copied. The reference's section 1 is the map.
- **Versions** (`P1`): "41" is written; **every version is read by the existence mask alone** (reference 3.6, pitfalls 17 and 24): every recording the owner has says "21" or "30" (reference 5.3). Another version is logged once per link, not refused.
- **Segments read:** mission (1), dwell (2), job definition (5), platform location (13). **Skipped and counted by type:** every other type, HRR, free text, test and status, processing history, job request and acknowledge, 101, 102 and the extension types among them (reference 3.6, 4.1). A free text is logged at debug.
- **Segments written:** packet header, mission, job definition and dwell (reference 4.3). No HRR, free text, status, history or platform location.

The dwell areas get a data type of their own, **`gmti.dwell-area`**, "GMTI dwell area" (owner, TG A: the model's type names are lower case with hyphens, so not `gmti.dwellArea` as first written), `"manual": false`, with `"fadeSetting": "/dwellAreaFade"`, so they fade apart from the platform and the coverage, which keep `gmti.platform`. **From M8 its defaults are shorter** (T-195): expires **15 s** after `time.observed` (the dwell's time; owner, 2026-11-16, YI A; 100 s at M8 RC1), not faded at expiry (the steps draw it), removed 0 s after expiry, so an area goes when `dwellAreaFade`'s defaults make it invisible. The map view draws the steps in place of the expiry fade (section 5).
- **The cap per dwell** (ADR-0048 point 3; CR-0031: default **1 000**, A5): reports beyond the link's `maxReportsPerDwell` are skipped and counted, over all segments of one dwell (pitfall 14).
- **A dwell with no reports** updates the platform and the area only (pitfall 13).
- **Skipped:** job 0 packets with dwells (malformed); `J5` 255 ends the job: the coverage goes (the dwell areas stay until they expire, T-195).

**Out** (reference 4.3 gives every field; decided here):

- Each `ChannelItem.SensorReport` becomes one packet: packet header (`P1` "41"; `P3` and `P5` the link's `nationality`, default `XN`; `P4` from the highest of the link's `label` and the item's detections' labels, mapped back to 1 to 5, so that **an empty dwell gets the link's label** (the ACA's R7); `P7` 1, or 129 when the link's `exercise` is on; `P8` from the sensor's name (below); `P9` from the sensor's mission (below); `P10` one job id per sensor, from 1), a mission segment, the job definition before the sensor's first dwell and at least every 30 s, and the dwell segment.
- **One segment per packet** (T-159; owner, MV A, 2026-11-09): the link setting `oneSegmentPerPacket` (section 5), **on by default** (owner, MY, 2026-11-09). **On,** each segment of the packet above goes in a packet of its own, in the same order: the mission, the job definition when it is due, then each dwell segment (more than one when the reports are split, below). Each packet has its own packet header, with `P1` and `P3` to `P10` as the one packet's would be, and `P2` its own size. Why: the owner's dissector reads only a packet's first segment; the guide's minimum receiver needs no more than one segment per packet (reference 3.7, Guide G.7.3). Over UDP each packet is one datagram. "In" reads both forms the same (reference 3.7). **Off,** one packet per sensor report, as above. The setting takes effect from the next report, without a reconnect.
- **The area** (reference 4.2): **an `ArcBand` by its own shape, wherever its centre lies** (T-193, owner TB A; PCA-gmti `120c06f6`): `D24`/`D25` its middle, `D26` half its depth, `D27` half its angle (AEDP 3.4.27), while `D7`/`D8` stay the report's platform at the report's time (AEDP 3.4.7). Before T-193 only a band centred within 1 m of the platform went so, and the simulator centred a revisit's bands where the platform was when the revisit began, so a moving sensor's band went as an "other shape" and came back as a wide sector or a near-full ring. **Since T-193 the simulator centres each dwell on the platform where its report puts it** (PCA-simulator `0b91a88d`; the UDA's M8 note); `gmti` still sends a band centred elsewhere by its own shape, as any sender may. "In" draws a band from `D7`/`D8` (reference 4.1), so its middle, far edge and width stay and its point moves to the platform; **a `Circle` centred on the sensor as 4607 allows** (Annex C-1.12; CR-0035, owner, KM, 2026-11-07): `D24`/`D25` the point halfway out at bearing 0° from the sensor, `D26` half the radius in km, `D27` 180°. That is the whole disc, which "In" (above; reference 4.1) reads back as the same circle round the sensor: inner 0, outer the radius. Other shapes are sent as the reference approximates them, and cut the same way from their near edge.
  - **The area's depth** (owner, JS and KA, 2026-11-07, with CR-0035's KM; G12): `D26` holds at most 255.99 km, so a dwell area reaches at most **511.98 km beyond its near edge**. **The out channels declare it: `maxAreaDepth(511_980)`** (CR-0035), which the simulator compares with to warn (its function 30). The near edge is the area's distance from the sensor at its nearest point: an `ArcBand`'s inner radius, and 0 for a `Circle` round the sensor.
  - **A deeper area is cut** (JS): the simulator's reach is generic and not cut (its function 28), so `gmti` cuts. Only the range is cut, the near edge stays (owner, KA, 2026-11-07, confirming PCA-gmti's Q5), and the bearings stay:
    - an `ArcBand` keeps its inner radius and reaches at most 511.98 km beyond it, **measured from the band's own centre** (T-193), not the platform, so its middle (`D24`/`D25`) moves to 255.99 km past the inner edge: a band from 20 to 1 000 km keeps 20 to 531.98 km;
    - a `Circle` round the sensor goes whole up to a radius of 511.98 km ("Detect all in range" at 400 km included); a larger one is sent with a radius of 511.98 km: `D26` 255.99 km, its centre 255.99 km out.
  - **What is left out:** a cut dwell counts `not held: D26` once (the ACA's R4: once per dwell), and **each detection outside the cut area**, farther from the sensor than the near edge plus 511.98 km, **is left out** and counted (`beyond reach`). So the packet never reports a target outside its own area, and leaves out exactly the tracks the simulator warns about.
    - `gmti` measures each detection's **reported** position from the report's `platform`, with the SDK's geodesy; the simulator measures its track's true position (the ACA's R2). They can differ by the position error at the edge.
    - Since the cut keeps the near edge, every cut area keeps its full 511.98 km, and the ACA's R4 case, a band wholly beyond the limit, cannot arise.
- **Reports:** full-precision positions; `D32.6` when the content has an altitude; `D32.7` the radial speed towards or away from the sensor from the content's speed and direction, else absent; `D32.10` the simulated class from the content's symbol (reference 4.3's list; a `vessel` symbol is 134); `D32.11` 100; `D32.12` and `D32.13` from the sensor (below). No truth tags in M5.
- **The sensor** (CR-0033; `SensorReport.sensor()`, `null` from a sender built before it, which gives "SIM" and seven hex digits of the sensor's id as the name and "no statement" for the rest, assumption B2):
  - **`P8`** (the ACA's R2; G2): the name with each character outside the basic set (`0x20`–`0x7E`) replaced by `?`, cut to 10 characters, padded with spaces. When another sensor on the link already holds it, the first free of the name cut to 8 plus `~2` to `~9`, then cut to 7 plus `~10` to `~99`. **A sensor keeps its `P8` from its first dwell while the link runs**, also when its track is renamed, so a receiver sees one platform per sensor.
  - **`M1` and `P9`** (CR-0034; G6):
    - **`M1`, the mission plan,** is the sensor's `mission` with each character outside the basic set replaced by `?`, **the same rule as `P8`'s** (the ACA's R3), cut to 12 characters and padded with spaces; all spaces when the sensor has no mission. A mission longer than 12 characters counts `mission cut` once per sensor while the link runs; a replaced character alone counts nothing, so a Swedish name is no cut (R3). No suffix is added: two missions that differ only after their twelfth character show the same `M1`, and their `P9`s tell them apart.
    - **`P9`, the mission id** (the ACA's R2): **CRC-32 (ISO-HDLC, the polynomial of `java.util.zip.CRC32`) of the mission's whole text as UTF-8**, before the replacement and the cut, as an unsigned 32-bit number, **0 mapped to 1**. The function is a contract between releases: the same mission gives the same `P9` across restarts of the link, across links and across releases, so a receiver sees one mission per sortie, with only the reference day moving when a restart falls on another day. With no mission, `P9` is the second of the day the link started, as before.
    - `M2`, the flight plan, stays all spaces.
  - **The values** (reference 4.3): `J15` the revisit in deciseconds; `J21` and `D32.12` the range error in cm; `J22` the cross-range error as an angle at the area's centre, and `D32.13` in dm; `J24` and `D31` the minimum detectable speed in dm/s; `J25` the detection probability in percent; `D15`–`D17` the platform's direction and speed, with vertical speed 0, only when both are given. A report with `D32.12` or `D32.13` makes the dwell's `D12`–`D14` 0 (AEDP 3.4.32.12). Each value is rounded to its field's unit first.
  - **A value its field cannot hold** (the ACA's R3; G10) is sent as "no statement" (`J21` 65 535, `J22` 180°, `J24` 255, `J25` 255) or left out (`D31`, `D32.12`, `D32.13`), **never clamped or wrapped**, and counted (`not held: <field>`). **`J15` has no "no statement" value** (G10): a revisit above 6 553.5 s is sent as 0, "not revisiting", the only value that claims nothing false, and counted. The simulator's settings are generic (owner, JQ, 2026-11-07; its function 28), not narrowed to 4607's sizes, so `gmti` does this mapping: its revisit of 1 to 600 s always fits `J15`; its errors and minimum speed may not fit.
  - **An absent value** is "no statement", as before.
- **Splitting:** at most `maxReportsPerSegment` (default **120**, A5) reports per dwell segment, more segments of the same indices after it; over UDP, the packet must also fit one datagram (reference 5).
- **A slow link drops older dwells** (the CR-0030 review's R7): the channel's inbox keeps the latest sensor report per sensor, so a link that cannot keep up loses whole reports; the link counts `dwells dropped` from the inbox's count.
- **Ground truth** is not taken: the channel's kinds are `SENSOR_REPORTS` only. **Relations are not sent** (ADR-0051 point 8).
- **The job area, the sensor type and a revisit's dwells** (M6, T-168 and T-169; CR-0039 point 7, CR-0040; the simulator's functions 36 to 39):
  - **Ground radars only:** the out channels declare `sensorTypes` `[GROUND_RADAR]`, so the simulator offers them no generic sensor. A report whose `Sensor.type` is another type is not sent, counted `not a ground radar`. A report without a type, from a sender built before 1.5.0 or without a `Sensor`, is sent as in M5.
  - **The job's bounding area** (`J6`–`J13`): from `Sensor.jobArea` when the report has one, its four corners **clockwise** as A to D (owner, PK A, 2026-11-10; AEDP-4607 3.7.6): A is the ring's first corner, and a counter-clockwise ring is reversed after A (the simulator's job area has exactly four, its function 39). **A job area that is not a convex area of four corners** (more or fewer corners, or a concave or crossed ring) is sent as the box round it, counted `not held: J6` once per sensor (owner, PK A); latitude first in the bytes, as the reference gives. Without one, the box round the dwell area, as in M5 (`plugins/gmti/README.md`, "Out"). The job definition goes before the sensor's first dwell, **again with the next dwell when the job area changes** (AEDP-4607 3.7: the actual area whenever it differs), and at least every 30 s, as before.
  - **A revisit's dwells** (CR-0040): with `SensorReport.Sequence`, `D2` is the revisit number modulo 65 536, `D3` the dwell number and `D4` its `lastOfRevisit`. Without one, as in M5: the link's own revisit count, `D3` 0 and `D4` set. The channel's inbox keeps the latest report per sensor and dwell number, so a revisit's dwells do not replace each other; `dwells dropped` still counts what a slow link loses.

## 4. Transport

- **Modes:** `tcp-client`, `tcp-server` and `udp`, each `in` or `out` (no `both`). A `tcp-server` and a receiving `udp` bind to loopback unless the admin chooses another address (CLAUDE.md section 4).
- **Framing by `P2`** (reference 5): over TCP read 32 bytes, then `P2` − 32; over UDP one packet per datagram, and a datagram whose length differs from `P2` is dropped. A file is the same stream.
- **Multicast** (A3): a `udp` `in` link joins the group `multicastGroup` on the interface `multicastInterface` when the admin gives one (for example the group 239.255.0.1, port 4142, from 239.255.0.0/16); an `out` link may send to a group.
- **Largest packet:** 65 535 bytes (`P2`'s range is the format's; reference 1.2). A larger or malformed packet is dropped and counted; over TCP the connection task reconnects, since a stream has no marker to find its place again (pitfall 18).
- **State across packets, in the worker per link:** the reference day per platform and mission; the job definition per job; dwells waiting for a mission segment (section 3); the reports of a dwell counted against the cap across its segments. No dwell areas (section 3, "No list of areas").
- **Writes:** through the `ObjectWriter` (one in flight per object; the latest wins), the platform, its areas and the detections alike (from M8 each dwell area a create of its own, T-195; again from M8 RC2, YI A); only the detections list the field set (section 3). A dense stream (simulator set B: 10 dwells a second, up to 531 reports each) is bounded by the cap and the writer's limits, each refusal counted.
  - **A moved object stays where the user put it** (T-155; SDK 1.5.0 part 5, CR-0039 points 10 and 11; ADR-0053 point 8; owner, Q1 A): a platform, its areas or a detection a user moved into another layer are updated there. The `ObjectWriter` does it from SDK 1.5.0 part 5: an update is built with the stored object's `layerId`, which the plugin's `Build` passes on unchanged, and the link's target layer is only where new objects appear; after a lost plugin document it adopts only a View layer with the link's layer name. A copy ("Copy to layer…") has no external identity (ADR-0053 point 3), so the plugin never updates it: it stays as copied, and the original goes on.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

The reference's test checklist (6.2) is part of these criteria: each line there is a unit test, with a point where latitude ≠ longitude.

1. **Angles and units:** Stockholm, Cape Town and Rio de Janeiro encode and decode as the reference 6.2 gives; `B16`, `BA16` and the times as there.
2. **Parsing by the mask:** a mandatory-only dwell (80 bytes), three reports, report bits with `D5` 0, report errors without `D12`–`D14`, versions "21", "30" and "41" all parse.
3. **Time:** a dwell before its mission segment waits and is placed on the right day; `D6` past a day goes to the next day; a new mission segment changes the day for later dwells only; `as received` shifts a link's times so its newest dwell is now.
4. **Platform and area:** a sensor at a known point (latitude ≠ longitude) with a centre 30 km away at 90°, `D26` 5 km and `D27` 7.5°, gives an `ArcBand` of 25 000 to 35 000 m between 82.5° and 97.5°; `D27` 180° gives a `Circle`; `D26` 0 hides the area. The platform moves with each dwell and each platform location segment.
5. **Detections:** three reports give three `gmti.detection` objects at their positions, each with the packet's label, the field set `gmti.report` and its details, the symbol of its class (Q8), and one `gmti.pairing` relation to the platform object; the same report twice is one object.
6. **The cap:** 500 reports in two segments of one dwell with a cap of 120 write 120 and count 380 over the cap.
