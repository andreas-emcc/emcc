# ACA review of CR-0035: an output channel states how far from the sensor it can carry detections

- **Reviewed:** `docs/change-requests/CR-0035-an-output-states-its-reach.md` on `main` at `8f516711` (PDA, for the owner; Q15 to Q17, JT, JU, JV, after JQ).
- **Against:** ADR-0037 point 4 (a channel describes itself); ADR-0048 points 6 and 7; ADR-0051 points 2, 3, 8 and 10; ADR-0052 (Map Pane overlays); `ChannelSpec` and `ChannelInfo` on `integration/sdk-v1.4` and the web SDK's `MapPaneOverlay` (SDK 1.3.0); `docs/architecture/gmti-4607.md` sections 1.3 (`D26`), 4.2 and 4.3; the simulator's functions 27 to 30 and the `gmti` brief's "Out".
- **By:** the ACA, 2026-11-07.

## Verdict

**Recommend approval.** The simulator needs to know what an output cannot carry, and the only generic way is for the output to say so (Q16): one number on the channel's self-description, as `kinds` and `fieldSet` already are, keeps the simulator free of any format (JQ). The bound is conservative for 4607 (point 2 below), which the ACA accepts as the owner's choice. The simulator's own relation kind is within ADR-0051 and needs no model change (Q17). No ADR changes. Five remarks, none blocking.

## The PDA's three points

1. **`maxReach` as a generic member, refused on ground-truth channels.** Right. ADR-0037 point 4 makes a channel describe itself, and SDK 1.4.0 already added what it takes (`kinds`, ADR-0048 point 4) and which field set it uses (`fieldSet`, ADR-0050 point 7); how far it carries a detection is the same kind of statement. The check in the compact constructor, as the record's other checks, and `ChannelInfo.of` carrying it, so that ECore's registry changes nothing, follow the records as they are. The constructors stay, so every 1.3.0 channel compiles. Metres, finite and positive, `null` for no limit, matches the SDK's units and its nullable convention.
2. **255.99 km is a conservative bound.** The reference's 1.3 has `D26` as B16 kilometres, so the area's half extent is at most 255.99 km. Measured from the platform that is exact for a `Circle` (the simulator's "Detect all in range": the radius is `D26`) and loose for a band: 4607 carries any band whose depth, outer minus inner, is at most 511.98 km, wherever it lies, so a band from 300 to 500 km fits, and the bound warns about it and cuts it to nothing. The real limit is 4607's own shape rule, and a generic member cannot state it without becoming 4607's. The owner chose the simple bound (Q15, JS, after JI); the ACA accepts it, with R1 and R4. The simulator's default band, 20 to 150 km, is untouched.
3. **`simulator.beyondReach` in place of `gmti.pairing` for those tracks.** Within ADR-0051: a plugin declares its own kinds (point 10) and writes relations as content (point 3); one plugin writes a platform's relations in M5, and in Simulation it is the simulator (points 3 and 8). The kind states a fact of the simulation, a detection that a chosen output cannot carry, not a colour, so it belongs among the relations. Replacing the pairing rather than adding a second relation keeps one line per track, and the map view draws the kind as any declared kind (ADR-0052). A style per relation would change CR-0031's model, the map view and every forwarder for one colour: rightly rejected.

## What the ACA checked beyond the CR

`ChannelSpec`'s and `ChannelInfo`'s checks and constructors on `integration/sdk-v1.4` (the compact constructor of `ChannelInfo` reuses `ChannelSpec`'s checks, so the refusal lands in one place); `MapPaneOverlay` in the web SDK, with its bottom corners; `D26`'s type and the area formulas in 4.2 (a `Circle` round the sensor sends `D26` as its radius, a band half of outer minus inner); the simulator's range band of 0 to 1 000 km (function 27, JQ) and its pairing rule (function 30).

## Re-check of R1 (2026-11-07, the MCA's request KG), against the aligned text and KA

- **Re-checked:** CR-0035 as the PDA aligned it at `682c9023` (`gmti`'s cut is the `gmti` brief's under JS and KA, not the CR's), and the brief's "Out" on `integration/sdk-v1.4`: `D26` is cut to 255.99 km **from the area's near edge**, the bearings unchanged; an `ArcBand` keeps its inner radius and reaches at most 511.98 km beyond it; a `Circle` round the sensor keeps its centre and gets a radius of 255.99 km; each detection outside the cut area is left out and counted (criterion 19).
- **R1 does not hold under KA.** It made the reach a distance from the platform and asked `gmti` to drop every detection farther than that. KA keeps the near edge instead, so `gmti` sends a band from 20 to 400 km whole, detections at 350 km included, and a band from 20 to 1 000 km up to 531.98 km. A reach of 255.99 km from the platform would then warn about tracks that `gmti` carries: in a 20 to 400 km band, every track from 256 to 400 km; in a 20 to 1 000 km band, every track from 256 to 532 km. The warning would be false wherever the band's inner edge lies out, and right only for a circle and for the default band of 20 to 150 km, where nothing is cut. R2 to R5 stand.
- **What `maxReach` should mean.** The ACA sees three readings, and recommends the first:
  1. **The depth beyond the area's near edge (recommended).** `maxReach` is the farthest a carried detection may lie beyond the near edge of the dwell's area, measured along the range from the sensor: the output keeps the area's near edge, cuts its far edge there, and leaves out the detections beyond, counted. That is KA's rule in generic words, since an area and its near edge are the SDK's `Dwell`, not 4607's. `gmti` declares 511 980 m, and the simulator warns about a track when its distance from the platform exceeds the area's inner radius plus the reach, exactly what `gmti` drops. For a `Circle` this holds once `gmti` represents it as 4607 allows (Annex C-1.12): `D24`/`D25` halfway out along any bearing, `D26` half the radius, `D27` 180°, which is the whole disc (the VISTA recording's half disc is the same with 90°), and which 4.1's formula reads back as the same circle (inner 0, outer the radius). Then a circle of up to 511.98 km goes whole, "Detect all in range" at 400 km included, and the circle's limit is the band's: one number, generic, exact for `gmti`. That is a `gmti` brief edit (its "Out" and criterion 19, with the reference's 4.2) and no SDK difference beyond the Javadoc. The name may stay `maxReach` if its Javadoc says this; `maxAreaDepth` says it better; the owner's call.
  2. **A distance from the platform, and `gmti` declares no limit (`null`).** No false warning, and no change to `gmti`; but no warning either, so Q15's intent is lost, and the tracks beyond 531.98 km in a 1 000 km band disappear with nothing but `gmti`'s counter to say so.
  3. **A distance from the platform at 255.99 km, kept by `gmti`.** The ACA's R1 as written. It reverses KA and drops detections that 4607 carries. Not recommended.

  A fourth way, the output telling its sender per dwell what it left out, would be exact for any output and any rule, but it is a new path from a channel back to its sender through the SDK: after 1.4.0, if a second limited output ever needs it.
- **Under the first reading** the CR's text changes in three places: point 1's definition and `gmti`'s value in point 3 (511 980 m, with the circle's representation), the simulator's comparison in point 4 (the distance less the area's inner radius), and the ITA's flow: a sensor with "Detect all in range" at 400 km to `gmti` warns about nothing, since it is carried whole; a band from 20 to 1 000 km, or a range of 600 km, warns about the tracks beyond 531.98 km and 511.98 km. The verdict stays: recommend approval, with the meaning fixed as the owner decides.
