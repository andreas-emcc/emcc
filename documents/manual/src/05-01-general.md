# General

**General** holds settings for the whole web application. At present it holds one.

## Default look

The look that every user gets who has not chosen one for themselves in the **Look** control in the top bar (see [Look](03-07-look.md)). Whether the look is light or dark follows each user's computer, unless the user chooses.

| Field | Choices |
|---|---|
| Look | Night Watch, Chart Room or Signal. The one in use is marked "(now)" |

Choose a look and click **Save**. The change applies at once to every user who uses the installation default. The names of the three looks and what each is made for are in [Look](03-07-look.md).
