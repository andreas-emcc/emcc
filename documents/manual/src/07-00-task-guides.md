# Task guides

A task guide takes you through one job from the beginning to the end, in the order you do it. It names each panel and each setting that you need, says what to type, and shows what the screen looks like after each step. The other parts of this manual describe one panel or one setting at a time and tell you everything about it. A guide tells you only what you need for its job, and points to the chapter that has the rest.

The task guides are part 7 of this manual. They are also one document of their own, **emcc Task Guides**, as a web page (`task-guides.html`, in the manual's folder) and as a PDF (`emcc-task-guides.pdf`), so that you can hand them to someone who only needs the guides. The PDF stands alone. The web page needs the manual's folder around it, with its pictures, style and pages, and its links to other chapters open those pages.

## How a guide is written

- **What you get.** The first lines say what the result will be.
- **What you need.** The permissions and the other things that must be in place first.
- **Steps.** Numbered, one action in each step. The text in **bold** is what you click or choose, and the text in `code` is what you type.
- **Where it goes wrong.** A table of what you see when something is not right, and what to do.

The values in a guide are examples that work together. Change them when you know why.
