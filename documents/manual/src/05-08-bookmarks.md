# Bookmarks

One setting for the [Bookmarks](03-06-bookmarks.md) control.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Most bookmarks and folders per user | How many bookmarks and folders one user may keep together. A whole number, at least 1. emcc also limits the size of the user's bookmark document (256 KiB), so a very large number may never be reached | 1 000 |

At the limit, **Add bookmark** and **New folder** are switched off for that user, with the reason.
