Mock-up · demonstration system

Extensible Modular Command and Control – a lightweight C2 (and simulation) system.

A shared live map for units, tactical graphics and tracks, with links to other systems and a simulator; extend the functionality with plugins.

Built by Claude Code agents, with humans as architects making every decision.

Source code on GitHub

HighlightsFour of the things emcc does

Highlights

  • Ground moving target radar.

    A simulated ground radar on an aircraft looks to its right: its coverage is the arc between two bearings. Dwell by dwell it reports the vehicles it sees in STANAG 4607, and emcc’s GMTI link draws them back as dwell areas and detections. Every name and position is fictional.

  • Security labels by design.

    Every object and layer carries a STANAG 4774 security label, by design since the first data model. The label designer in Settings prepares premade labels per policy; using them to control access comes later.

  • Full motion video.

    STANAG 4609 video comes in over UDP: the camera and its field of view on the map, and the stream in a window with its classification. MPEG-2 video is converted to H.264 on the server. The video shown is our own test stream, a simulated camera over public-domain height data. From M9, emcc's own UAV simulator sends such video too.

  • A UAV simulator.

    Put a camera on a track in the Simulation workspace, point it at a place, an object, a fixed angle or a sweep, and emcc makes live video from it: drawn from orthophotos and height data that you add, with the metadata a real camera sends, and sent out as STANAG 4609 to any receiver, emcc itself included. Here a simulated UAV circles Sundsvall's harbour; the orthophotos are Lantmäteriet's of 2001.

Simulation: a second map that moves

  • Draw routes and put units on them one by one, as a convoy or in a formation.
  • Give each point a speed or a time, an altitude, and events that change a unit's fields or hide it.
  • Add ground radar sensors that report what they detect.

Standards

What emcc reads, writes and follows. Standards are named only to say that; emcc is not certified against any of them.

Data links

Cursor on Target (CoT)In · Out
In and out, over TCP or TLS, UDP and multicast; the simulator sends its tracks through it too.
AIS (NMEA 0183)In
Ships from AIVDM sentences over UDP or TCP.
ADS-B (SBS-1)In
Aircraft from a decoder's BaseStation text stream on TCP port 30003.
STANAG 4607 (GMTI)In · Out
Ground moving target radar data, AEDP-4607 Edition A; the simulator's sensors send it too.
STANAG 4609 (FMV)In · Out
Full motion video over UDP: H.264, H.265 or MPEG-2 video with MISB ST 0601 metadata in a transport stream; MPEG-2 converted to H.264 on the server. Out: the H.264 video of the UAV simulator's cameras.
NVG 2.0In · Out
NATO Vector Graphics, in and out: files, and NVG web services.

The ideas behind it

  • A live picture, shared.

    The server holds the master copy of every layer and object. Every browser shows the part its user looks at, and every change reaches everyone who needs it.

  • Change requests.

    Objects change only through change requests to the server, which checks each one against the user's permissions. The browser only hides what a user may not do.

  • Layers and security labels.

    Every object is in a layer. Layers are drawn in order, and can be shown, hidden and locked. Every layer and object carries a security label after STANAG 4774.

  • Data types and data sources.

    An object's data type (General, CoT, AIS vessel, ADS-B aircraft, Route) decides how long it stays current. Its data source says where it came from: placed by hand, or the name of a link.

  • Field sets.

    Plugins add their own fields to objects, such as a CoT unit's team or an AIS ship's details, and pass on every field they do not understand.

  • Symbols, shapes and tactical graphics.

    APP-6 unit symbols and vessels; circles, ellipses, rectangles, polygons, arrows and routes with hatched fills and arrowheads; boundaries, phase lines, axes of advance, objectives, minefields and more.

  • Terrain.

    Range and bearing between two points, the ground an observer can see, and a height map: the ground inside a circle in 16 colour bands, with its highest point marked. From DTED height data that you add.

  • Links to other systems.

    Cursor on Target (CoT) in and out; ships from AIS; aircraft from ADS-B; ground moving target radar data in STANAG 4607 format, in and out; NATO Vector Graphics (NVG), in and out; full motion video in STANAG 4609, in and out. Background maps from Natural Earth, OpenStreetMap or WMS servers.

  • Positions.

    Always WGS84. Shown and typed as degrees, minutes and seconds, decimal degrees, UTM/UPS or MGRS, polar regions included.

  • Maps in any projection.

    Web Mercator, and polar maps of the Arctic (EPSG:3413) and the Antarctic (EPSG:3031).

  • Your own workspace.

    Movable panels, saved setups, bookmarks, three looks (Night Watch, Chart Room, Signal) in light or dark, and display settings for symbol size, labels and speed vectors. They follow the user to another computer.

  • Login and permissions.

    Login through OpenID Connect, with groups that decide what each user may see and do; or a no-login mode for demonstrations.

  • Works without internet.

    The browser talks only to the emcc server, and Natural Earth maps ship with it. Online maps are optional.

The parts

How emcc is put together.

In the browser

The web shell (top bar, workspaces, panes and panels, looks, login) and the web parts of the plugins. TypeScript; the map is drawn with OpenLayers and WebGL.

On the server

One Java 25 process.

  • A thin Spring Boot edge: HTTP, the WebSocket of the live picture, login.
  • ECore, the core:
    • the plugin host
    • the worker runtime with its watchdog
    • the message bus
    • the object store
    • the live picture gateway
    • the persistence manager
    • the video streams and their conversion
    • security
    • settings
    • logging and metrics
  • The server parts of the plugins.

Outside

The systems emcc talks to: TAK systems over CoT, NVG systems, video from UAVs, AIS and ADS-B receivers, ground radars, WMS map servers, and the Keycloak integration for login.

21

Plugins

Everything beyond the core is one of 21 plugins, each with a server part, a web part or both, built on a Java and a TypeScript SDK. Plugins never create threads: ECore runs their workers, connection tasks and timers, and reports any that hang.

Maps

  • Plugin · map-view-2d

    2D map view

    The map in the middle of every workspace, drawn with OpenLayers and WebGL. It pans and zooms, draws symbols, shapes and graphics, selects and drags them, and shows clusters when zoomed out over many objects. It switches between Web Mercator and the polar projections.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-map-view-2d.

  • Plugin · basemap-naturalearth

    Natural Earth background

    An offline background map in every projection, polar ones included. Land, lakes, coastlines, ice shelves and borders from Natural Earth 1:50m, muted so that the symbols stand out. It ships with emcc and needs no internet.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-basemap-naturalearth.

  • Plugin · basemap-osm

    OpenStreetMap background

    OpenStreetMap tiles in Web Mercator, for development and demonstrations. The emcc server fetches the tiles, so the browser talks to no other host. Disconnected installations use WMS servers instead.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-basemap-osm.

  • Plugin · basemap-wms

    WMS background maps

    Background maps from WMS servers, polar projections included. The server part reads each server's capabilities and passes checked map requests on. A background is offered only in the projections that its server offers.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-basemap-wms.

Drawing

  • Plugin · draw-symbols

    Draw symbols

    Place, edit and move APP-6 unit symbols and vessels. The Symbols panel chooses and places them; Symbol properties edits the selected symbol, including the field sets that other plugins add.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-draw-symbols.

  • Plugin · draw-shapes

    Draw shapes

    Draw circles, ellipses, squares, rectangles, areas, arrows and routes, and edit them later. Each shape has a style, a name and a data type, with hatched fills and arrowheads where they fit.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-draw-shapes.

  • Plugin · draw-tactical

    Draw tactical graphics

    Place APP-6 tactical graphics: boundaries, phase lines, axes of advance, areas, fire support areas, range fans and more. Choose the identity, the status and the echelon, from a short list of common graphics or the whole catalogue.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-draw-tactical.

  • Plugin · symbology

    Symbology

    The one place where APP-6 symbols are understood and drawn, for every other plugin. Unit symbols are drawn with milsymbol, and tactical graphics with mil-sym-ts in a Web Worker.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-symbology.

Organising

  • Plugin · layer-panel

    Layers

    The layer list: show, hide, lock and reorder layers, and give each its classification. It lists the layers of the workspace shown.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-layer-panel.

  • Plugin · object-list

    Objects

    The objects of one layer as a list, page by page, also for a layer that is hidden on the map. Its rows share the map's selection and its actions.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-object-list.

  • Plugin · bookmarks

    Bookmarks

    Saved map views in a folder tree: the projection, the background, the centre and the zoom, back in one click. They are each user's own and follow the user to any browser.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-bookmarks.

  • Plugin · coordinates

    Coordinates

    Positions shown and typed as decimal degrees, degrees, minutes and seconds, UTM/UPS and MGRS. Everything inside emcc stays WGS84 longitude and latitude; only what the user sees and types changes. Other plugins use its service.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-coordinates.

Links

  • Plugin · cot

    Cursor on Target

    Receives CoT from TAK systems and shows it on the shared map, and sends the objects of chosen layers to them. Its links run over TCP or TLS, UDP and multicast, and the simulator sends its tracks through them too.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-cot.

  • Plugin · ais

    AIS

    Ships from AIS receivers, as NMEA 0183 sentences over UDP or TCP. It keeps one symbol per ship at its last position, with its name, course and speed. Input only.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-ais.

  • Plugin · adsb

    ADS-B aircraft

    Aircraft from an ADS-B decoder's SBS-1 stream on TCP port 30003. It keeps one symbol per aircraft at its last position, with its callsign, track and ground speed. Input only.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-adsb.

  • Plugin · gmti

    GMTI

    Ground moving target radar data in STANAG 4607, in and out. Incoming data shows the radar platform, its coverage and its detections on the map; the simulator's sensors send their reports in the same format.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-gmti.

  • Plugin · nvg

    NVG

    NATO Vector Graphics 2.0, in and out. It imports NVG files and polls NVG web services, and places their symbols, tactical graphics, shapes and texts on a layer. It exports a layer as an NVG file, or serves it as an NVG web service for other systems.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-nvg.

  • Plugin · fmv

    FMV

    Full motion video in STANAG 4609, over UDP. It shows each camera as a symbol and its field of view as an arc on the map, lists the streams in the FMV panel and plays them in a window with their classification. MPEG-2 video is converted to H.264 by the server; H.264 and H.265 pass unchanged. Its output links draw the video of the UAV simulator's cameras and send it.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-fmv.

Analysis and training

  • Plugin · terrain

    Terrain

    Range and bearing between two points, and a viewshed: the ground that an observer can see, from DTED height data that you add. Range and bearing need no height data.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-terrain.

  • Plugin · simulator

    Simulator

    The Simulation workspace: routes with speeds, altitudes and events, convoys and formations, and ground radar sensors. The server moves the tracks once a second, even with no browser open, and sends them out through the links.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-simulator.

  • Plugin · uav-simulation

    UAV simulation

    Simulated cameras on the tracks of the Simulation workspace, as on a UAV, in the Cameras panel: each tracks a point or an object, holds fixed angles or sweeps a sector. For every frame it works out where the camera is and where it looks, and an FMV output link turns that into video.

    Built and kept by its own agent, PCA-uav-simulation.

Built by AI agents

emcc is an experiment in running a software project with AI agents as the team.

Claude Code agents in 12 roles (an architect, a core developer, a plugin designer, one coder per plugin, an integration tester, a manager, reviewers, a service agent, a user-experience designer and writers) wrote almost all of the code, the tests and the documents. Humans as architects wrote the requirements and made every decision. The agents work only through files in git, each in its own part of the repository.
