Natural Earth vector map data, version 5.1.2, scale 1:50m: land, Antarctic ice shelves, lakes,
coastline and country borders on land. Used by the basemap-naturalearth plugin; converted in
format and simplified, not edited in content (CR-0009).

Natural Earth data is in the public domain. No permission is needed to use, change or
distribute it.

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.
